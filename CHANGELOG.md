# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-01)

### Added
- First public release.
- FPV drone races for Cortex Drone: host or join a lobby at a track, with laps and an optional entry fee.
- Countdown on the start lights, gates counted by the server, instant respawn at the last gate, and a live race HUD.
- Results with every pilot's line, a speed heatmap, an altitude trace and an in-world replay.
- Time trials against your personal best ghost, and a leaderboard per track.
- An in-game track creator with 17 pieces, and the LSIA Neon Circuit demo track.
- Entry fees and prizes on Qbox or QBCore, with a money ledger that refunds after a restart.
